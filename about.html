HOW WE WORK

The order of operations matters.

Most phone problems have more than one possible cause. Working in a fixed order is what keeps a repair honest.

01Diagnose first

Hardware and software are tested before a part is ordered or fitted.

02Quote before work

You hear the cost and scope, and you agree, before anything is opened further.

03Repair carefully

Screws, shields and adhesives belong where they came from. Your phone is a personal device, not a job number.

04Test after

Display, touch, charging, audio, camera and sensors are re-checked before handover.
