HOW WE WORK

The order of operations matters.

An accessory is only right if it fits and does what the picture promises. Working in a fixed order keeps that honest.

01Confirm the fit first

Tell us the exact model, and we check the listing fits that phone before money changes hands.

02Quote before ordering

Price and availability come from the shop's own list, confirmed on WhatsApp before you commit.

03Check your artwork

For custom prints we review resolution and colours with you before anything goes on a machine.

04Test at the counter

Glass is aligned, grips are stuck, prints are inspected — in front of you, before handover.
